Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Account details (name, email, optional phone), your orders and delivery notes, feedback you submit, and messages sent through the contact form.

How we use it

To create your account, process and deliver orders, send status updates, improve the service and answer your messages. We do not sell your data.

Analytics and cookies

If enabled, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It sets cookies and sends anonymous usage data to Google. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Who can see your data

You can see your own orders. Authorised BharatVera staff can see orders and feedback to fulfil them. Service providers that host our data and send our emails process it on our behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while your account is active or as needed to run the service. To ask for access, correction or deletion, use the contact form.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change.
