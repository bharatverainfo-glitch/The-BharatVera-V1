Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Open the app, log in, choose Place Order, pick Food or Grocery, list your items and confirm.

How can I track my order?

Open My Orders and tap an order. The timeline shows each stage with its time.

Can I cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. The store can cancel an order until it has been delivered.

How do I leave feedback?

Once an order is Delivered, open it, choose 1 to 5 stars, add a note and save. You can edit it later.

Will I get updates about my order?

Yes. When the status changes you receive a message with the new status.

What do you do with my data?

We use your details to run your orders. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.
